Our Story

From seed to cup — a labour of love.

Brewhaus began in 2018 as a single espresso cart in Brooklyn. Founders Maya and Tom had one obsession: exceptional coffee made with integrity. Every bean is ethically sourced, every recipe meticulously crafted.

Today, Brewhaus is a full kitchen and coffee bar — but the obsession hasn't changed. We still taste every roast, talk to our farmers, and treat every customer like a regular.

Why Brewhaus

Not just coffee. A daily ritual.

We built Brewhaus to be a third place — not home, not office, but somewhere equally welcoming. A space to think, connect, and find your rhythm.
